## Full description (max 4000)
How many times have you picked up your phone to check the time and put it down twenty minutes later?

Sordina is an Android launcher that puts your phone back in its place: you pick it up because you need it, you do the thing, and it goes quiet again. What matters gets through right away; the rest waits until you go looking for it. It works from the first minute with nothing to set up, and you can change anything later. ("Sordina" is Italian for a trumpet mute.)

★ A HOME THAT DOESN'T CALL YOU
Instead of a grid of icons: the date, the weather, your next plan and one bar to type or say what you need. At the bottom sits the sordina, a little brass mascot that tells you only what matters right now.

★ NOTIFICATIONS WAIT FOR YOU
Everything stays muted, except what you choose:
• Important: verification codes, banking, missed calls, urgent messages
• The apps you let through, whole or without the notification types you don't need
• The people who matter, everywhere or on one app only
Everything else waits on the Sordina page, grouped by person, until you decide to look. Nothing gets lost. And if someone writes three times in five minutes, it gets through anyway.
Need some quiet? "Mute for 45 minutes", then everything resumes.

★ ONE THING AT A TIME, READY TO TAP
The mascot's bubble hands you what you need right now:
• the verification code to copy, with its countdown
• the meeting about to start, with "Join" for Teams, Meet, Zoom and Webex
• your ride, your parcel, a payment, the song you're playing
• the doorbell or courier photo: swipe up and see it without opening the app

★ SAY IT, AND IT'S DONE
"Call Anna." "Text Mark I'm on my way." "Wake me up tomorrow at 7." "Football Thursday at 8pm." "Turn off the living room lights."
Sordina does the right thing without making you hunt for the app: it calls, writes on WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal or SMS, sets alarms and timers, adds the event to your calendar, starts navigation. Voice is recognised on the phone, and commands work in all 27 languages of the app.

★ A SPEED BUMP FOR "JUST BECAUSE" APPS
Apps you open out of habit ask for a few seconds before opening, and so do their websites in the browser. Give each one a daily budget if you like. The apps you need open straight away.

★ HOME CHANGES WITH YOU
• Evening: the day winds down with one line, time on the phone and unlocks, no charts
• Night: only the essentials, a dark screen
• Car: a big clock, navigation, music, the people you picked and a microphone you can hit without looking

★ SMALL WINS, NO PRESSURE
Achievements and mascot stickers celebrate the choices you made. No notifications to keep a streak alive: what you win stays yours.

★ HOME ASSISTANT, IF YOU HAVE IT
Lights, shutters, scenes and questions from the same bar. Routines like "Leaving" or "Coming home" in one tap, even together with your alarm. Opening doors, gates and garages or disarming the alarm asks for your fingerprint first. Without Home Assistant, Sordina works just as well.

★ EVERYTHING STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
No account, no ads, no tracking, no Sordina servers. Notifications, contacts, calendar and what Sordina learns stay on the phone. The only things that go online are the weather (Open-Meteo, location rounded to about 10 km) and, if you connect it, your own Home Assistant. The code is open source (GPL-3.0): anyone can check it.

★ MADE WITH CARE
Light, dark or automatic. A solid, abstract or your own wallpaper, with text that always stays readable. Phones and tablets, 27 languages. Free, with no in-app purchases.

Permissions: notification access decides what gets through and what waits; usage access orders apps in search and powers the evening recap. The accessibility service is optional: it powers the instant brake, double-tap to lock the screen and typing phrases into Home Assistant or Signal. Sordina explains it and asks for your consent before turning it on. None of this data leaves your phone.
