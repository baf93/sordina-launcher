## App name (max 30)
Sordina – Calm Launcher

## Short description (max 80)
The phone that doesn't interrupt you: what matters gets through, the rest waits.

## Full description (max 4000)
You pick up your phone because you need it, you do the thing, and it goes quiet again. Nothing invites you to stay, nothing important slips by.

Sordina is an Android launcher that puts your phone on mute — "sordina" is the Italian word for a trumpet mute. It isn't a grid with fewer icons: it starts from what you want to do and the moment you're in.

★ A CALM HOME
Date, weather and your next plans. One bar to type or say what you need. At the bottom, the little brass mute mascot tells you only what matters right now. Nothing else.

★ NOTIFICATIONS WAIT FOR YOU
Important people, calls, verification codes and urgent messages get through right away. Everything else stays muted: you'll find it on the Sordina page, grouped by person, whenever you choose. Nothing gets lost and nothing disappears on its own.
• Simple rules per person, per app or per notification type
• Every waiting notification tells you why it's waiting
• Pictures from notifications show up there too
• "Mute for 45 minutes": timed silence, then everything resumes

★ WHAT YOU NEED, WHEN YOU NEED IT
The mascot's speech bubble hands you one thing at a time, ready to tap:
• the verification code to copy, with its countdown
• the meeting about to start, with "Join" for Teams, Meet and Zoom
• your ride, your parcel, a payment, the music you're playing
• questions from your smart home, with their answers
Swipe sideways to move to the next thing. If there's a picture, swipe up to see it.

★ INTENT FIRST, APP SECOND
"Call Anna", "text Mark I'm on my way", "timer 10 minutes", "football tomorrow at 5pm", "turn off the living room lights". Type or speak: Sordina does the right thing without making you hunt for the app. Voice is recognised on the phone.

★ HOME CHANGES WITH YOU
• Evening: the day winds down, a one-line recap, no charts
• Night: only the essentials, a dark screen
• Car: a big clock, your navigation and music apps, the people you picked and a microphone you can hit without looking
Each mode turns on and off whenever you like.

★ SUGGESTIONS THAT LEARN, ON YOUR PHONE
Sordina learns what you usually do at this time and place and offers it, one thing at a time. All the reasoning happens on the phone. Long-press to see why it's suggested, or to never see it again.

★ SLOW DOWN "JUST BECAUSE" APPS
Apps you open out of habit ask for a moment before opening. The ones you need open straight away.

★ HOME ASSISTANT (OPTIONAL)
Connect your home: voice or typed commands, and your home's questions right in the bubble. Sordina works great without it too.

★ EVERYTHING STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
No account, no ads, no tracking, no Sordina servers. The only network requests are the weather (Open-Meteo, with a location rounded to about 10 km) and, if you connect it, your own Home Assistant. The code is open source (GPL-3.0).

★ MADE WITH CARE
Light, dark or automatic, with solid, abstract or your own wallpaper: text always stays readable. Available in 27 languages.

Sordina is free, with no in-app purchases.

Note: Sordina uses notification access to decide what gets through and what waits, and usage access for suggestions and the evening recap. This data never leaves your phone.
